# Tonight, We All See Him Off

[![Excerpt from the published group-farewell film.](../assets/showcase/jigong.gif)](https://www.douyin.com/video/7689014871601832057)

Ji Gong is a beloved fictional monk in Chinese popular culture, known for his ragged robes, fan, humor, and compassion. This imagined farewell begins with a friend opening a door, then opens out to neighbors seeing him off along a lane.

## The directing decisions

| Decision | Its job in the scene |
| --- | --- |
| Begin with one person's greeting | Give the collective farewell a personal beginning |
| Let doors and windows open along the route | Make care visible through other people's actions |
| Use a particular smile and fan gesture | Preserve the character's familiar manner |
| Move from a close encounter to a departing figure | Change the emotional distance through framing |
| Leave the neighbors watching after he walks away | Give the absence time and space |

The creator particularly praised the expressions, fan gestures, and music in feedback on the farewell films. These were shared observations about the films, not separate measured contributions to reach.
