# There's a Foreigner! Let's Take a Photo

[![Excerpt from the published video.](../assets/showcase/sydney.gif)](https://www.douyin.com/video/7693037053788959673)

Chinese holidaymakers fill the Sydney Opera House waterfront. An Australian local becomes the unusual sight everyone wants a photo with. The explanation that China is on holiday leaves him wondering whether he is still in Australia.

## The directing decisions

| Decision | Its job in the scene |
| --- | --- |
| Keep the landmark recognizable | Establish the place before the social reversal |
| Treat crowd density as part of the joke | Make the local becoming a minority visible |
| Bring nearby tourists into the photo | Turn the crowd into people taking action |
| Leave room for the local's puzzled response | Let a character complete the joke |
| Keep the crowd and place present during the exchange | Preserve the visual premise through the dialogue |

An early returned version had only a small group and too much empty space. Its prompt also specified too few tourists and moved into tight framing. The revision used a full-scene reference and kept foreground, middle-distance, and distant crowd layers readable. That is a concrete staging and input correction, not just a request for “more cinematic” imagery.

Try: “Use $ai-video-director to develop a social role reversal in a recognizable place. Make the environment carry the premise and let a character's reaction land the joke.”
