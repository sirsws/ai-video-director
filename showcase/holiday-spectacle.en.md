# A holiday crowd becomes a spectacle

Two travel works develop a familiar inconvenience into a fantasy the protagonist can join. This page discusses the production designs and creator-reported response; no video preview is presented here.

| Work | Everyday starting point | Designed transformation | Recorded response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National Day on Wugong Mountain | A mountain path packed with holidaymakers | A trekking pole becomes a sword; the crowd becomes martial artists moving toward a mountaintop stronghold | 29,000 views, 936 likes, 891 remakes |
| National Day in Dunhuang | A camel tour stuck in dense traffic | A selfie stick becomes a spear; the tour groups become cavalry responding to a departure order | 26,000 views, 924 likes, 891 remakes |

Figures were reported by the creator on October 8, 2026, in a Jimeng discussion; the platform was not restated for each row. They are not attributed to Douyin or WeChat Channels.

## What carries the transformation

The starting crowd is already the raw material for the final spectacle. Preserve its depth, density, and geography through the change. Match the prop grip and movement across the transition. Give the newly transformed crowd something to do: the protagonist's order should produce a visible response across the space.

Music also has a designed role: playful travel accompaniment changes into an energetic martial-arts song for the mountain work, and into a driving battle score for the camel work. These are design choices, not an independent listening verdict on the results.
